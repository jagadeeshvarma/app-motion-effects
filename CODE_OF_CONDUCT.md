# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to participate respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

Be considerate and constructive. Critique the work, explain evidence and respect different levels of experience. Accept correction and keep discussion relevant to the project. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualised language or imagery toward contributors.

These expectations apply in issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behaviour and consider context.

Report concerns using GitHub’s private “Report content” controls on the relevant content or profile. Do not publish personal details in an issue. If a concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub’s reporting process rather than sharing sensitive information publicly.

This is an original concise policy, not a claim of adoption or certification by another conduct framework.
